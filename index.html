Reservoir Network Thinning

Can aggressively thinned reservoir networks improve reconstruction of chaotic dynamics?
Approach
More than 100 million numerical experiments compared thinned and conventional reservoirs.
Finding
Highly thinned networks often outperformed conventional networks; the report proposes spectral-radius guidance.

RISK Game Prediction

Question
Can a simulated strategy game's eventual winner be predicted before the game ends?
Approach
Tens of thousands of PyRisk games produced board-state features for models at different stages of play.
Finding
Accuracy rose as games progressed; the report cautions that the data represents AI players, not humans.

Movie Revenue Modeling

Question
How well can public movie features predict domestic and worldwide box-office revenue?
Approach
The team compared several machine-learning methods and reported separate choices for each market.
Finding
K-nearest neighbors led domestically and backpropagation worldwide, with selection bias clearly noted.
