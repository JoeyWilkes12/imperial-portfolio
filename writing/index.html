Write the Decision Before the Dashboard

Start with the decision

A useful analytical brief can begin with one sentence: who must decide what, by when, and what would change their mind? That sentence gives every metric a job.

Make evidence earn its space

The smallest useful view often contains a current state, a meaningful comparison, and enough context to act.

Leave a decision trail

Capture assumptions, exclusions, freshness, and ownership beside the analysis.

The Smallest Useful Automation

Find the repeated handoff

Automation is most persuasive where a repeated handoff already has a clear input, an identifiable owner, and an observable definition of done.

Prefer a thin vertical slice

A small end-to-end path exposes real constraints earlier than a wide collection of disconnected utilities.

Design the escape hatch

Human review, reruns, and readable logs are part of the product. The best early automation is easy to inspect and safe to interrupt.

Designing Technical Handoffs That Survive

Build for the second reader

A handoff is successful when someone outside the original build can explain the system, operate it safely, and know where its boundaries are.

Separate map from manual

The map explains components and relationships. The manual explains recurring operations.

Name the next owner

Ownership is a design property. Record the person or role responsible for data definitions, runtime health, access, and future changes.
