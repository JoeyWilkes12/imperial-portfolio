# Imperial 2.0 Product Context

## Product

Imperial 2.0 is Joey Wilkes's public portfolio for data science, artificial
intelligence, engineering, analysis, and research work. It is a standalone,
no-build static website and a parallel draft of the existing Imperial site.

## Audience

The primary audience is hiring managers and technical collaborators. They are
evaluating the quality of Joey's work, the evidence behind it, and how quickly
they can reach a representative project. Search engines, recruiters, scrapers,
and AI agents are secondary audiences and must receive complete semantic HTML.

## Primary action

A first-time visitor should select a project, understand its question,
approach, and reported finding, then follow the source or project-detail link.
The first viewport prioritizes exploration over biography.

## Content truth

The four projects, four experience entries, three sample article outlines,
three recommendations, public profile links, and all visible metrics come from
the incumbent Imperial source. The portfolio does not invent claims. Project
and experience provenance remains visible in the interface.

## Direction

The committed concept is TRACE: a working lab book rendered as cool drafting
paper, ruled hairlines, compact evidence, and one red recording-pen accent. A
live Lorenz-attractor trace connects the interface to the reservoir-computing
project. The signature interaction is an evidence ledger: four project rows
select a compact Question / Approach / Finding panel.

## Voice

Direct, precise, compact, and evidence-led. Home-page language is functional.
Long-form language belongs on article and project detail pages.

## Interaction and access

The site progressively enhances complete HTML. It supports keyboard, touch,
fine-pointer hover, reduced motion, JavaScript-disabled reading, 390px mobile,
and 1440px desktop. Motion explains selection and the Lorenz trace; it never
delays access to content.

## Visual constraints

- No gradients, glass surfaces, pill clusters, oversized marketing hero, fake
  terminal, generic feature-card grid, or decorative AI imagery.
- Zero-radius ruled geometry, cool paper, near-black ink, muted pencil text,
  and one red accent.
- One display/UI sans family and one monospaced data family, both with robust
  system fallbacks.
- Icons are inline SVG or text glyphs. The interface does not depend on an icon
  library.

## Success checks

- All four projects appear in or immediately adjacent to the first desktop
  viewport.
- A visitor reaches a project source in two interactions or fewer.
- The page remains complete and intelligible with JavaScript disabled.
- No horizontal overflow at 390px or 1440px.
- Navigation, ledger selection, disclosures, external profiles, and internal
  detail links pass automated browser checks.
- Reduced-motion users see a static trace and no animated transitions.
